MAINTENANCE LTD

Heritage Construction and Refrigeration Maintenance Ltd is a multi-disciplinary contractor providing comprehensive building, asset management, and climate control solutions. The company specializes in high-quality property construction, renovations, and preventative building maintenance alongside expert industrial and commercial refrigeration services.

This includes the professional installation, emergency repair, and routine maintenance of HVAC, air conditioning, and cooling systems delivered by accredited trade engineers.
